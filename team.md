## The University of Texas at Austin

The project is led by **Ibrahim Volkan Isler** with UT Austin senior/key personnel including **Joydeep Biswas, Roberto Martin-Martin, Peter Stone, Yuebing Zheng, and Yuke Zhu**.

## Research Collaboration

The Phase I project includes collaboration with **UL Materials Discovery Research Institute (MDRI)** and **Medra AI**, with laboratory workflows grounding the robotics research in scientific experimentation.

## Texas Robotics

SAFE-BOLT is developed within the broader robotics ecosystem at The University of Texas at Austin, with access to multiple robotic platforms and large-scale computing resources for simulation and learning.
