## Vision

SAFE-BOLT aims to enable robot lab assistants that can be instructed to perform repetitive, physically demanding and safety-critical portions of scientific experiments within existing laboratory workflows.

## Why SAFE-BOLT?

Many laboratory procedures remain difficult to automate because they require dexterity, contact reasoning and safe interaction with tools, fixtures, liquids, glass and other challenging materials. SAFE-BOLT focuses on dual-arm robots with dexterous hands and force-aware execution.

## Phase I

Phase I establishes the technical foundation through digital twins, human demonstrations, simulation-based policy learning, safety-constrained real-world control, robust perception and cross-platform validation.

## Core capabilities

- Bimanual and dexterous manipulation
- Contact-rich alignment, insertion and fastening
- Human demonstration and structured task narration
- Simulation-based learning and sim-to-real transfer
- Force-aware closed-loop control
- Runtime safety monitoring and recovery
- Robust perception for reflective, transparent and difficult laboratory materials
