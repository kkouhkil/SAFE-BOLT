## 01 · Digital Twin

Transfer the laboratory environment, robot and task-relevant objects into simulation using geometric and interactive models.

## 02 · Human Demonstration

Record task demonstrations together with structured subtask narration, procedural context and explicit success conditions.

## 03 · Policy Learning in Simulation

Retarget demonstrated motion to bimanual robots and train policies to achieve narrated subtask goals despite differences in morphology and contact configuration.

## 04 · Safety-Constrained Control

Decompose procedures into manipulation primitives such as align, insert, squeeze, seal and fasten, then execute them with closed-loop controllers and explicit force or safety constraints.

## 05 · Real-World Feedback

Use system identification and multimodal perception to reduce the sim-to-real gap and trigger online corrections when physical execution diverges from the expected state.
