## Materials Synthesis & Assembly

A representative workflow combines liquid and powder dispensing with mechanical assembly. Relevant robotic skills include grasping, alignment, insertion, placement, sealing, turning and fastening. The SAFE-BOLT assembly demonstrator provides a focused setting for developing these contact-rich capabilities.

## Microscope-Centered Optofluidic Workflow

A second representative workflow includes microliter pipetting, handling glass and films, and interacting with instrument controls. These operations emphasize precision, careful force regulation and perception under reflections and transparent materials.

## Shared Manipulation Primitives

The project studies reusable primitives across workflows and robotic platforms, including align/insert, press/seal, pipette actuation and turn/fasten.
